Background

Audit experience and operations experience — in one seat

I have spent a decade-plus on both sides of the file: risk-based audit, internal controls, financial reporting, and quality work at large global and national public-accounting firms, and in-house internal audit for public companies. On the firm side that meant integrated audits of SEC filings (10-Qs and 10-Ks), financial statement audits, SOC 1 Type 2 examinations, model validations, and more. Inside the company it meant financial, operational, IT, and compliance audits.


Part of my work has been on how teams get things done: governed use of tools, standard approaches, and broader rollouts. I have provided innovative, practical solutions and led adoption of AI in audit and advisory work across industries, trained teams, and put new tools into governed, reviewable workflows working cross-functionally. Leaders get one seat on the project, with professional standards, quality, and risk in view.
